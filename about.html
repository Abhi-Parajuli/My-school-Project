Who We Are
Education Built on Community Values

Bhanudaya Secondary School (भानुदय माध्यमिक विद्यालय) was established as a community-based educational institution in Dumkibas, Nawalaparasi, Nepal. Affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB) and approved by the Ministry of Education, we serve students from ECD through Grade 12.

We offer Plus Two programs in Management and Education streams, with moderate fee structures and scholarship schemes for deserving students. Our aim is to make quality education accessible to every family in the Nawalpur district.
